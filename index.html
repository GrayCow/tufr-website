About TUFR

"What we lack in experience, we make up for with passion and determination."

Trinity University Formula Racing is a student chapter of Formula SAE, the world's premier collegiate engineering design competition. Our team designs, builds, and races a formula-style car from the ground up. Every system is engineered entirely by students, from suspension geometry to body panels.
