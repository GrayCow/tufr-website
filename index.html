Who we are

About TUFR

"What we lack in experience, we make up for with passion and determination."

Trinity University Formula Racing is a student chapter of Formula SAE, the world's premier collegiate engineering design competition. Our team designs, builds, and races a formula-style car from the ground up — every system engineered entirely by students, from suspension geometry to body panels.

Where you fit in

Any major. Any year.

No experience required — just curiosity and a willingness to learn hands-on.
